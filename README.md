# ⚡ Cyber Breakout: Quantum Brick Breaker

A fast-paced, vibrant, retro-futuristic cyberpunk brick breaker game built with pure HTML5 Canvas, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript. Features smooth 60fps paddle-deflection physics, dynamic explosive TNT bricks, falling power-ups (Multi-Ball, Dual Laser Blasters, Paddle Expander, Laser Floor Barrier), and procedural Web Audio sound synthesis with zero external libraries or assets!

## ✨ Features

- **🎮 Quantum Brick Types:**
  - 💎 **Cyan Core (1 Hit):** Standard quantum energy matrix (+10 pts).
  - 🛡️ **Shielded Violet (2 Hits):** Heavy armor plating (+25 pts).
  - 💣 **TNT Nova (Cluster Explosive):** Detonates all neighboring bricks with screen shake (+50 pts).
  - ⭐ **Prismatic Mystery (Power-Up Drop):** Guaranteed power-up capsule (+30 pts).
- **💥 Falling Power-Up Capsules:**
  - 🟢 **Multi-Ball (M):** Triples the number of active balls on screen!
  - 🔴 **Dual Lasers (L):** Mounts dual blaster cannons on your paddle to disintegrate bricks.
  - 🔵 **Wide Paddle (W):** Expands paddle defense perimeter by 50%.
  - 🟡 **Laser Floor Shield (S):** Activates a bottom safety laser wall that deflects falling balls.
- **🎵 Real-Time Procedural Web Audio API:**
  - Melodic pitch-scaling brick combo chords.
  - Deep low-frequency TNT explosive rumble.
  - Laser zap audio effects.
  - Power-up pickup musical chime.
  - Sound mute/unmute toggle.
- **📱 100% Mobile Responsive & Touch Friendly:**
  - Full mouse tracking on desktop.
  - Smooth finger touch drag tracking on smartphones and tablets.
  - Keyboard arrow keys / A & D + Spacebar support.
- **🏆 LocalStorage High Score System:**
  - High score automatically persisted under `cyber_breakout_best`.
- **🚫 Zero Dependencies:**
  - Single-file `index.html`.
  - Zero external frameworks, remote fonts, audio files, or AI API keys required.

## 🎮 How to Play

1. Move your paddle with **Mouse Cursor**, **Touch Dragging**, or **Arrow / A & D Keys**.
2. Press **Spacebar** or **Tap Screen** to launch the ball (and fire lasers when active).
3. Catch falling glowing power-up capsules with your paddle.
4. Clear all cyber bricks to advance to higher sectors!

---

## 🛠️ How to Play Locally

Double-click `index.html` to run immediately in Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox.
